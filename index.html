Eeyore lives on your device.

Pressing Download model fetches a small language model (SmolLM2-360M-Instruct, about 270 MB with WebGPU, about 370 MB on the CPU) once. Your browser keeps it, so later visits start almost instantly. After that the conversation runs entirely on your device: nothing you type is sent to a server. It uses your graphics card through WebGPU when it can, and your processor otherwise (slower, but it gets there).
